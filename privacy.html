Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 13, 2026

Welcome to Toolify. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it when you use our website and services.

1. Information We Collect

When you sign in using Google Authentication, we may receive basic profile information including:

  • Your Name
  • Your Email Address
  • Your Profile Picture
  • Your Google Account ID

This information is used solely to provide account functionality and personalize your experience on Toolify.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Authenticate and manage your account
  • Personalize your dashboard
  • Display your profile information
  • Store your preferences and settings
  • Improve the functionality of Toolify
  • Provide support when needed

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

3. Local Storage & Cookies

Toolify uses browser storage technologies such as Local Storage, Session Storage, and Cookies to remember:

  • Theme preferences (Dark Mode / Light Mode)
  • User settings
  • Session information
  • Application preferences

You may clear this data at any time through your browser settings.

4. Third-Party Services

Toolify uses Firebase (Google) for authentication, database services, and hosting.

Learn more about Google's privacy practices:

Google Privacy Policy

5. Rule-Based Chatbot

Toolify includes a chatbot called MohakGPT.

MohakGPT currently operates as a rule-based chatbot. Responses are generated using predefined rules and programmed logic, not human review.

Please avoid sharing sensitive information such as passwords, banking details, government identification numbers, or other confidential data through the chatbot.

6. Data Security

However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.
