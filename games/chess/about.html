What is CheckMate?

CheckMate is a fully-featured online chess platform. Whether you want to sharpen your tactical vision with daily puzzles, battle a challenging AI, or play real-time chess with a friend anywhere in the world — this is your board.

Every rule of chess is enforced precisely: castling, en passant, pawn promotion, stalemate, and check detection. The AI uses a real minimax engine with alpha-beta pruning and piece-square tables — not random moves.
